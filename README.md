# Flying Gorilla

Play: https://mcgregorai2026-dotcom.github.io/flying-gorilla/

A jet-pack gorilla platformer (6 worlds, 6 bosses, co-op). Single-file game (index.html) packaged as an installable PWA.

- Android Chrome: open the link, then menu > "Install app" / "Add to Home screen". The installed app opens fullscreen in landscape.
- In a normal browser tab it plays in portrait or landscape and handles rotation.
- Works offline after the first visit. New versions load automatically next time you open it online (the page is network-first).

What's new in v12: The Crash Site. A short cartoon movie intro plays the first time you press Play (an alien ship crashes in the jungle; replay it any time with Watch Intro on the Main Menu). Level 1 is now The Crash Site: fight through the burning jungle, punch open the crashed saucer, ground-pound 3 buttons to free Pip the robot drone, punch the glass capsule to find your jet pack, then escape through the roof at sunrise. On a new game the JET button is a JUMP button until you find the pack (existing saves keep theirs). Pip follows you everywhere with a light for dark places; find drone parts in 6 levels (or buy them in the new Drone shop tab) to unlock the Scanner, Helper Zap (a short stun, no effect on bosses) and Hints. In co-op each player gets a drone.

What's new in v11: Jet pack progression. The starting Classic pack is now a short boost (stretch a jump, cross a small gap, slow a fall) so platforming is the main way through. Beat each world's boss to find a better pack in its treasure chest (Crystal, Magma, Frost, Storm, Neon); the top packs (Steam Punk, Rainbow, Golden) cost hundreds to over a thousand coins. Every pack shows Lift / Fuel / Refill bars in the shop. Each level has a soft sky ceiling (canopy, cave roof, ash, snow clouds, storm clouds, force field) so you can't fly over the level. Fuel cans now give TURBO fuel until you land. Packs you already bought are kept.

What's new in v10: Mango's Jet Shop. Spend the coins you collect on 12 jet pack styles, upgrades (Banana Magnet, Coin Magnet, Bigger Fuel Tank, Jet Recharge, Extra Heart) and power-ups (Shield, Super Punch, Double Coins, Mega Magnet). Old saves keep their coins.
