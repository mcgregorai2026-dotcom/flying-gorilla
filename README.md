# Flying Gorilla

Play: https://mcgregorai2026-dotcom.github.io/flying-gorilla/

A jet-pack gorilla platformer (6 worlds, 6 bosses, co-op). Single-file game (index.html) packaged as an installable PWA.

- Android Chrome: open the link, then menu > "Install app" / "Add to Home screen". The installed app opens fullscreen in landscape.
- In a normal browser tab it plays in portrait or landscape and handles rotation.
- Works offline after the first visit. New versions load automatically next time you open it online (the page is network-first).

What's new in v18: Big polish pass! JET and POUND buttons swapped places, bonus rooms got themed walls, bunting and a ceiling (no more flying out into empty sky), Level 1 has a moon, stars, fireflies and flowers, checkpoint flags show a banana badge, and lots of little glitches are fixed (floating scenery, snow blobs, stray ferns in boss arenas, a bonus-room hiccup after falling, the menu signpost hiding behind the title).

What's new in v17: Double-tap is back! On touch screens, double-tap the MOVE side to JUMP. With a jet pack, double-tap and hold MOVE to fly while you steer; let go to stop. (This replaces the v15 lift-and-press-again rule.)

What's new in v16: The waterfall on Jungle Island now pours off a real rocky cliff! The water spills over a mossy rock lip at the top, tumbles down the cliff face, and splashes into a pool with foam and soft mist before flowing into the river.

What's new in v15: Easier jumping and flying on touch screens. No more double-tap: just lift your thumb off the MOVE side and press it again (within about a second) to JUMP. With a jet pack, keep holding after that press to fly while you steer; let go to stop. Your first press after a pause always just walks, so starting to walk never makes you jump. The MOVE circle, How to Play and the first tutorial sign explain it.

What's new in v14: Longer levels! Every regular level is now about twice as long, with new set pieces (moving platforms, crystal caves, climbs, geysers, ice slides, airships, laser halls, bonus rooms) and extra checkpoints along the way. Every level can still be beaten with the jet packs you find, and there are no timers. Shop prices went up (early items are affordable in World 1, the top packs take saving across many levels); everything you already bought is kept. New in Settings (Main Menu and pause): Controls lets you put the MOVE stick on the LEFT and the buttons on the RIGHT. Double-tap the MOVE side to JUMP before you have a jet pack, or double-tap and hold to fly once you do. The world map got a makeover with a framed title, glossy level pads, a sunny sky, sparkling sea, floating motes and big welcome signs.

What's new in v13: A rebuilt crashed spaceship in Level 1. The flying saucer now lies tilted and half-buried in a smoking crater (cracked glass dome, flickering lights, sparks, glowing goo). Inside is one simple path through 4 rooms: punch open the jammed airlock, wake Pip in the dark hallway, ground-pound 3 glowing buttons in the power room, then smash the glass capsule to find your jet pack. A goal line at the top always says what to do next, Pip points the way and glowing arrows on the floor lead on. The camera is steadier, speech bubbles stay clear of the ape and the buttons, and the escape is a calm jet-hop up a short shaft (no timer) out into the sunrise. The intro movie's saucer matches the crash.

What's new in v12: The Crash Site. A short cartoon movie intro plays the first time you press Play (an alien ship crashes in the jungle; replay it any time with Watch Intro on the Main Menu). Level 1 is now The Crash Site: fight through the burning jungle, punch open the crashed saucer, ground-pound 3 buttons to free Pip the robot drone, punch the glass capsule to find your jet pack, then escape through the roof at sunrise. On a new game the JET button is a JUMP button until you find the pack (existing saves keep theirs). Pip follows you everywhere with a light for dark places; find drone parts in 6 levels (or buy them in the new Drone shop tab) to unlock the Scanner, Helper Zap (a short stun, no effect on bosses) and Hints. In co-op each player gets a drone.

What's new in v11: Jet pack progression. The starting Classic pack is now a short boost (stretch a jump, cross a small gap, slow a fall) so platforming is the main way through. Beat each world's boss to find a better pack in its treasure chest (Crystal, Magma, Frost, Storm, Neon); the top packs (Steam Punk, Rainbow, Golden) cost hundreds to over a thousand coins. Every pack shows Lift / Fuel / Refill bars in the shop. Each level has a soft sky ceiling (canopy, cave roof, ash, snow clouds, storm clouds, force field) so you can't fly over the level. Fuel cans now give TURBO fuel until you land. Packs you already bought are kept.

What's new in v10: Mango's Jet Shop. Spend the coins you collect on 12 jet pack styles, upgrades (Banana Magnet, Coin Magnet, Bigger Fuel Tank, Jet Recharge, Extra Heart) and power-ups (Shield, Super Punch, Double Coins, Mega Magnet). Old saves keep their coins.
